SleepLess keeps your Mac awake — lid open or closed — from a small menu-bar app.

This installer puts SleepLess into your Applications folder and sets up its helper: the small root job that lid-closed mode and the charging light need. macOS asks for your password (or Touch ID) once, at the Install step. Nothing will ask again — later versions update themselves, signed, from inside the app.

SleepLess then opens in your menu bar. It runs on Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later. If SleepLess is already running, it is quit and replaced; your settings are kept.
